# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone in Guild's issues, pull requests and discussions.

Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people, and assume the other person means well. Give feedback that helps someone fix the problem, and take feedback the same way.

Not allowed:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Sexual language or imagery aimed at someone
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing a thread

If someone breaks these rules, I'll edit or remove what they posted, warn them, or block them from the repository, depending on how serious it is.

To report something, email [biemgamer@pm.me](mailto:biemgamer@pm.me). I'll keep your report private.
